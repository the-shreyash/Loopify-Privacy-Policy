# Loopify Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 21, 2026

Loopify is a Chrome extension that provides repeat controls for YouTube videos and playlists.

## Information Collection

Loopify is designed not to collect, sell, or transmit personal information to external servers.

## Local Storage

Loopify may use Chrome's local storage to save preferences such as repeat mode, control style, and enabled status. These preferences remain in the user's browser.

## YouTube Access

Loopify interacts with supported YouTube pages to provide repeat playback and, when enabled, an optional repeat button inside the video player. Page access is used solely to provide the extension's functionality.

## Data Sharing and Tracking

Loopify does not include advertising or analytics and does not sell or share user data with third parties.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the extension developer through the support page listed on the Chrome Web Store.
